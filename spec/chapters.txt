# The chapters of the spec, in reading order (compiler/spec/README.md).
#
# A chapter line gives the chapter's rule-ID prefix, its file (relative to spec/) and its title.
# An indented line under it gives a construct of that chapter and the subject it covers: the
# second segment of a rule ID such as `stmt.bind.shadow.same-scope`. Reuse a construct before
# adding one. The order of the chapter lines is the reading order, the coverage order and the
# order the grammar is assembled in.
#
